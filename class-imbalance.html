Dealing with Class Imbalance in Classification

TL;DR

  • Accuracy is useless when one class is rare. A model that always says "not fraud" scores 99% on a 1% fraud problem. Look at precision, recall, PR-AUC and MCC instead.
  • Fix your evaluation first: stratified splits, an untouched test set, and any resampling done only inside the training folds.
  • Moving the decision threshold away from 0.5 is usually the cheapest and most effective fix. With costs, the optimal threshold is $C_{FP}/(C_{FP}+C_{FN})$, assuming calibrated scores.
  • Class weights and focal loss change what the model pays attention to during training. They are cheap and usually beat resampling.
  • SMOTE and friends sometimes help, often do nothing, and occasionally hurt.
  • Resampling and weighting distort predicted probabilities. If you need real probabilities, correct for the prior shift or recalibrate on untouched data (calibration).

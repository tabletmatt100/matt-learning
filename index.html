Viewing Options on Your Boox Go 10.3

Direct & Easy Methods to Read These Files:

Method 1: Direct System Browser (Fastest)
In your Termux terminal, run: 

termux-open ~/matt-learning/index.html

This will immediately trigger the Android app picker or open your tablet's browser (e.g. NeoBrowser or Chrome) with the file.

Method 2: Local HTTP Server (Best for tabbed browsing)
Run this in Termux to launch a lightweight offline server: 

./serve.sh

Then open http://localhost:8080 in any browser on your Boox Go 10.3 (or http://10.0.0.179:8080 from another device on your Wi-Fi).

Method 3: Copy to Device Documents Storage
If you prefer opening files from the Onyx Boox native Storage / Documents or NeoReader app: 

cp -r ~/matt-learning/*.html ~/matt-learning/katex /sdcard/Documents/

You can then open the files directly inside your Boox Library or Document viewer.
